Product Philosophy & Execution Framework

How I Think About Product & Problems

Drawing from 4+ years on the frontlines of Trust & Safety, fraud analysis, and risk operations at Wipro and IBM, I map my core principles across the Product Lifecycle using structured breakdown (Context, Challenge, Execution, Outcome):

Phase: Discovery & Framing
01

1. Root-Cause Problem Framing

"Treating a symptom without understanding the underlying risk vector leads to repeated failure. I isolate the exact root cause before writing PRDs."

Context: Early funnel drop-offs (e.g. Discovery to Menu) cause substantial user leakage.

Challenge: Identify why users abandon browsing and frame the exact root cause.

Execution: Analyzed stage-wise conversion drops and prioritized high-leverage intent fixes.

Outcome: Shaped Zomato and Fireflies strategies around exact user friction points.

Phase: AI Architecture
02

2. AI with Human Guardrails

"Unvalidated automation creates critical blind spots. AI products require strict confidence thresholds and human-in-the-loop verification."

Context: Fraud rules, recommendations, and automated transcripts required high reliability.

Challenge: Deploy automated pattern detection without degrading user trust.

Execution: Built confidence thresholds, AI recommendation rails, and fallback validation protocols.

Outcome: Successfully deployed robust risk detection rules and personalized recommendation flows.

Phase: Execution & Delivery
03

3. Cross-Functional Rigor

"Products fail in communication. I bridge engineering complexity, business strategy, and support realities from day zero."

Context: Misalignment between support operations and dev squads.

Challenge: Align engineering execution with frontline operational realities.

Execution: Provided crystal-clear acceptance criteria and metrics matrices.

Outcome: Streamlined team workflows and faster SLA resolutions.

Phase: Scale & Optimization
04

4. Scale with Security

"Rapid growth is meaningless if platform integrity is compromised. I evaluate roadmaps through a risk-aware lens."

Context: Scaling user acquisition and discounting created potential margin/vulnerability risks.

Challenge: Balance rapid feature shipping with strict data privacy and margin compliance.

Execution: Implemented guardrail metrics and robust cohort retention models.

Outcome: Protected user experience and business reputation during expansion.
